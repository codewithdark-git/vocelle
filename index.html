How it works

Three steps. No prompt engineering.

  1. 01

    Hold Right Ctrl

    Click into any text box first. A small indicator shows Vocelle is listening.

  2. 02

    Say what you mean

    Talk the way you would to a person. Silence and background noise are skipped.

  3. 03

    Release

    Your words appear at the cursor. Your clipboard is put back as it was.

Why Vocelle

Spend your words on the work, not the prompt.

Works offline

Speech recognition runs on your PC with open-source Whisper models. No internet needed after the model download.

Private by design

Audio is processed in memory and never leaves your computer. No account, no analytics, no telemetry.

Types anywhere

Word, Chrome, Slack, Teams, VS Code, Notepad: if it has a text cursor, Vocelle can type into it.

Stays out of the way

A small tray app with a floating indicator that never steals focus from what you're doing.

A model for every PC

Six models from Tiny (78 MB) to Large Turbo (574 MB). Vocelle recommends the right one for your hardware.

Verified downloads

Models come from the official whisper.cpp repositories and are checked with SHA-256 before use.

Requirements

Built for Windows.

  • Windows 10 (1903 or later) or Windows 11, 64-bit
  • A CPU with AVX2: Intel 4th gen (2013) or newer, AMD Ryzen or newer
  • 4 GB RAM (8 GB recommended for larger models)
  • A microphone
  • About 200 MB of disk space with a small model

First install

The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may show "Windows protected your PC". Click More info, then Run anyway.

Vocelle starts in the system tray. Open Settings, download the model marked Recommended, and you're ready.

FAQ

Questions

Does Vocelle send my voice to the cloud?

No. Transcription runs entirely on your PC. The only network requests Vocelle makes are the model downloads you start in Settings.

Which languages does it support?

English-only models are the fastest and most accurate for English. The multilingual models cover 99 languages.

Can I change the talk key?

Not yet. The talk key is Right Ctrl in this release; a configurable key is planned for the next one.

Nothing happens when I hold Right Ctrl.

Check that Vocelle is running in the tray and that a model shows ready in Settings. Some keyboards and remote-desktop tools remap Right Ctrl.

How do I uninstall it?

Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Vocelle → Uninstall.
